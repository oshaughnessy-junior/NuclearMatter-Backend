## Units, closure and branch prescription

Both routines take local T in kelvin, Fermi wave numbers in fm^-1, dimensionless **Landau** effective-mass/bare-mass ratios, pairing critical temperatures in kelvin and integer zone indices. They return local neutrino energy emissivity in erg cm^-3 s^-1. There is no gravitational redshift, volume integration, spectral neutrino bin, number source or transport opacity. Do not supply MeV temperatures, MeV Fermi momenta, Dirac effective masses or a radiation blocking distribution to this interface.

For unpaired matter the matched recipe is

```
alpha = 1.76 − .63(1.68/kFn)^2; beta=.68; t9=T/1e9
Qn = 8.55e21 mn*^3 mp* [(kFe+kFmu)/1.68] alpha beta t9^8
Qp = 8.55e21 mn* mp*^3 [kFe/1.68]
     (kFe+3kFp−kFn)^2/(8kFe kFp) alpha beta t9^8.
```

The historical aggregate formula is used exactly as written. In particular, the active proton term is marked `From Oleg` in the Fortran source; a different muon-containing expression is commented out. **Only Qn explicitly contains kFmu.** This parity does not silently add a proton-muon branch, a polygon step, a different angular-region expression or new weak-current physics. Neither reaction-resolved flavor/antiflavor sources nor a complete warm inventory can be recovered just by interpreting this aggregate emissivity as an arbitrary-degeneracy collision integral.

Selected normal inputs use nB=.12,.24,.48fm^-3 and prescribed Yp=.04,.08,.14. At zero temperature the free electron/muon chemical potential is solved from n_e(mu)+n_mu(mu)=n_p, with physical electron/muon rest masses and spin2 densities. Saved momenta satisfy charge neutrality and, where muons are present, mu_e=mu_mu. The **nuclear beta-equilibrium relation is not imposed**: no cold nuclear EOS/chemical potentials are part of this scalar port test. These are matched prescribed local phase-space inputs, not a newly self-consistent stellar EOS family. The muon threshold is handled in generating cold densities, not as a warm FD spectral threshold in the emissivity formula.

Pairing follows the source recipe: neutron1S0 for i>=isf and neutron3P2 B for i<isf; proton1S0. At **exact T=Tc**, strict `<` means unpaired. Both simultaneous suppression factors are combined by the same branch-wise minimum, not an independently computed coupled gap integral. The NMB inactive-branch guards and near-Tc tau clamp are retained. Tests at tau=.1,.3,.7,.99 and exactlyTc do not qualify uniform derivatives or arbitrarily close sub-Tc agreement, where the deliberate clamp must be considered. No PBF zone-boundary convention or other cooling process is tested here.

## Failed/excluded gates: port agreement is not physical accuracy

1. [Gusakov2002 Eqs14–15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0204334) explicitly gives a proton polygon step, zero for kFn>3kFp+kFe, and different angular expressions in separate momentum regions. The legacy routine applies a squared polynomial without that step. At kFn2.01,kFp=kFe.5fm^-1, original-code branch inference gives **Qp2.92606147789e16erg cm^-3 s^-1**, a3.91257e−5 fraction of the total, when the primary cold polygon requires zero. The unchanged3e−12 physical-zero gate FAILS. Both NMB and the Fortran recipe share this extrapolation; it is not a port mismatch. This study does not repair the rate or claim the same polynomial is physically valid on both sides. Some larger-Yp test compositions also enter a different primary angular region; passing port parity there is expressly a recipe test.
2. At kFn.8,kFp=kFe.4fm^-1, the fitted alpha becomes−1.0183 and **both codes return Q=−6.42164781293e20**. A nonnegative physical-emissivity gate FAILS. This is an excluded low-kFn extrapolation of the shared alpha fit, not evidence for negative physical emission. The zero alpha occurs at kFn=1.68sqrt(.63/1.76), approximately1.005fm^-1; a numerical fit must not be extended indiscriminately below it.
3. For kFe=0 with nucleons present, the original routine divides by its unguarded electron factor and terminates under FPE trapping; NMB's guarded expression returns finite values (muon-only7.45990898832e20 in the prescribed-momentum diagnostic, no-lepton0). Exact stdin, stderr and abnormal return codes are saved. These are outside the qualified electron-present comparison domain; no finite original-code comparator is invented. This is a **guard/convention difference**, rather than a normal-state rate discrepancy. No claim is made that such non-beta-equilibrated diagnostic momenta represent the matched neutral states above.

Excluded prescribed-momentum diagnostics explicitly omit inherited density/chemical metadata so they cannot be mistaken for the base charge-neutral state. No physical gate is loosened by calling agreement code parity.
