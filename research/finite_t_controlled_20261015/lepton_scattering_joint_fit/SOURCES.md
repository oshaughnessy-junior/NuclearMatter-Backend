# Physical source and new numerical construction

- [Guo et al., arXiv2006.12051v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.12051v2), leptonic scattering Eqs1–4,13 and AppendixA40–41, underpin the already reviewed massive-electron current, target-spin and thermal occupation conventions. The detailed model is the frozen campaign `neutrino_lepton_scattering/scatter.py` (SHA8628fb1fe917a6d84caf6aa789a50feab359790db1c8216eb936c8e0dcfa4064), with prior independent review in `review_neutrino_lepton/`. This fit adds no new current or radiation closure.
- `reference.py` invokes actual CM event quadrature for BOTH electron and positron targets, with final charged-lepton holes and unblocked outgoing neutrino. The reference and all three transitive physical source hashes are pinned. The separate `phase_reference.py` analytically integrates target azimuth and solves the transverse-shell delta reduction; it uses the same physical interaction model but a different integration reduction.
- New preregistration, training-only joint Chebyshev selection, frozen coefficients and fresh validation belong exclusively to this leaf. Frozen v1 fits, reference data and failures remain intact. Eighteen old v1 training/validation references are explicitly reclassified as known development for this new model; their old classifications/results remain unchanged in v1.
