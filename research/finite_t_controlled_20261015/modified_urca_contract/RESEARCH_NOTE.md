# Finite-temperature modified Urca: reference contract and next implementation

This leaf supplies an interaction-dependent implementation contract, a frozen NMB source audit, reaction bookkeeping, and a normalized degenerate energy benchmark. **It supplies no full modified-Urca rate, collision width, opacity, interaction-calibrated fit or production change.** Current direct-Urca phase-space references remain the detailed zero-width baseline. Independent review is pending.

## Verified primary-source choices

[Suleiman, Oertel & Mancini, arXiv:2308.09819v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.09819v2) use nonrelativistic mean-field nucleons and one-pion exchange at two-loop order. Their Fig1/AppA–C enumerate self-energy D/E and vertex V1/V2/V3 contributions, with exchange and isospin traces; Eq31 introduces a phenomenological intermediate-propagator regulator Gamma. Their computed observables distinguish true inverse mean free path from j+chi (Eqs9–11). This is a valuable reproducible reference prescription, not a regulator-independent exact warm rate. Important transcription gate: displayed Eq23 in BOTH HTML and PDF has identical momentum indices in the numerator, while its denominator has a transfer. Do not literally implement that zero numerator or silently claim an independently verified correction. The intended momentum-transfer operator must be checked against the interaction definition/source before numerical amplitude qualification. Eq28 normalization must likewise be audited together with its appendices, not lifted without their measure conventions.

[Alford, Haber & Zhang, arXiv:2406.13717v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13717v1), Eqs6–10, define a different approximation: a momentum-independent imaginary scalar mass iW/2 and a Lorentzian mass-spectral convolution of a mean-field direct rate. Their example uses IUF matter and imported widths; the convolution holds chemical potentials fixed. Their width-inclusive result is a TOTAL charged-current prescription, so adding a separate modified-Urca result would duplicate collision contributions. It omits vertex corrections and is explicitly not a sufficient neutral-current prescription. It is not arbitrary sigmoid smoothing. Our use would require new off-shell-mass domain and spectral-tail checks, with an explicit strong-interaction input, rather than automatically wrapping a positive-Dirac-mass direct-kernel API.

[Shternin, Baldo & Haensel, arXiv:1807.06569](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.06569), Eq2, explicitly use a full spin sum and collision symmetry factor1/2. Eq4 contains amplitudes before and after strong scattering and exchange; their intermediate propagator and half-on-shell interaction matter. It is a cold degenerate reference, not arbitrary-temperature validation. [Friman & Maxwell1979](https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1979ApJ...232..541F), especially SecIV/Eqs57–61, provides the historical separated energy/angular limit; its angular/current assumptions must be retained for a normalization comparison. We do not infer a warm amplitude from its T8 scaling.

The width provenance matters: [Sedrakian & Dieperink2000, astro-ph/0002228v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0002228), Eq69, gives gamma=aT²[1+(omega/(2piT))²] with a approximately.2MeV^-1, in a restricted pure-neutron Brueckner calculation. Their discussion limits its density/temperature use. A universal proton/neutron width T²/(5MeV) is therefore not established for merger compositions or arbitrary EOSs. Distinguish this microscopic-model uncertainty from integration error; audit full-width/half-width and energy-to-lifetime conventions before importing numerical values.

## Our process and thermodynamic contract

For each spectator N=n,p and charged-lepton flavor l=e,mu, keep FOUR reversible families:

| Family, forward convention | Radiated species | Signed material l number |
|---|---|---:|
| N+n -> N+p+l−+anti_nu_l | anti_nu_l |+1|
| N+p+l− -> N+n+nu_l | nu_l |−1|
| N+n+l+ -> N+p+anti_nu_l | anti_nu_l |+1|
| N+p -> N+n+l++nu_l | nu_l |−1|

These are16 reaction pairs/32 directed reactions across both spectators and both flavors; the executable inventory records every species change. Bookkeeping does not assert nonzero phase space. A spectator can exchange energy/momentum, so do not reuse the isolated free-proton/neutron three-body vacuum veto as a universal in-medium branch veto. In the degenerate soft-neutrino limit enforce the full five-Fermi-momentum polygon; the proton-spectator condition pFn<=3pFp+pFl can fail. An extra spectator does not mean every composition has an open T0 channel.

Use actual finite-T density roots for n,p and signed lepton chemical potentials. Neutrality is np=ne,net+nmu,net. Ye and Ymu are independent composition coordinates; specify their values or their equilibrium constraints. Photons, trapped neutrino species and nuclear composition are separately declared closures. Full reaction affinities use total chemical potentials: A_l=mu_n-mu_p-mu_l for anti emission, with the opposite sign for nu emission. The spectator chemical potential cancels. Trapped LTE requires mu_anti_l=A_l and mu_nu_l=-A_l. Empty-radiation stationarity requires an actual summed source balance and cannot be inferred from this identity alone.

Each epsilon_i(p), mu_i, kinetic mass, vector offset and rest convention is explicit. For an NR reference use epsilon_i=m_i+U_i+p²/(2mLi), or a consistently declared alternative offset; for RMF use epsilon_i=sqrt(p²+mDi²)+Ui. Landau and Dirac masses are not interchangeable. Use THE SAME dispersions in occupations, exact energy deltas and intermediate propagators. Keep participant DeltaU in physical energy conservation and distinguish physical weak transfer q from kinetic q*. A cold barotropic table or compact thermodynamic fit alone does not supply the required collision interaction, quasiparticle widths or weak vertices.

## Amplitude and phase-space contract

A next implementation must label its interaction model explicitly. A minimal historical comparator is leading-NR charged current and a declared antisymmetrized OPE NN operator, optionally with a separately parameterized short-range term. In a fixed plane-wave convention the intended OPE transfer structure is proportional to

    -(f_piNN²/m_pi²) (sigma1·q)(sigma2·q)/(q²+m_pi²) (tau1·tau2).

This defines a MODEL CHOICE to be verified, not a silently repaired transcription of Eq23. A form factor/cutoff and a short-range operator require their own parameters and provenance. They are not reconstructed from the cold aggregate coefficient. Weak magnetism, pseudoscalar, exchange currents and relativistic effects remain explicit omissions of the initial NR rung; importing the complete one-body direct current does not automatically produce a consistent two-body current.

At amplitude level include weak insertion before and after strong scattering on every participating leg, antisymmetrize identical nucleons, and retain exchange interference. Schematically H^mu=AS sum_a[T_NN G0 J_a^mu+J_a^mu G0 T_NN], supplemented by interaction currents if the chosen power counting requires them. M=GF Vud/sqrt(2) L_mu H^mu. This is a structural formula, not an implemented normalized squared amplitude. The initial deliverable must publish every labelled diagram, relative sign, spin/isospin matrix convention and denominator. Squaring diagrams separately and then summing is not generally the coherent amplitude square.

In an explicit proposed Pauli basis (n,p), take tau3=diag(+1,-1), so tau_minus maps n to p and tau_plus maps p to n; use separate literal matrices for reciprocal transitions. Keep the full tau1·tau2 strong operator, including charged-isospin exchange, rather than assigning a guessed spectator multiplier. Spin matrices are ordinary Pauli matrices. A first NR comparison may declare gV=1,gA=1.26 and quasiparticle residues Zn=Zp=1; historical-source comparisons must match their own GF,Vud,axial and normalization conventions. This is deliberately distinct from the already accepted relativistic one-body full-current model with its own axial value and F2/GP terms. Record all direct/exchange amplitudes before spin summation. Individual diagram/interference contributions need not be positive; require positivity of the complete physical squared amplitude, never floor negative interference terms. Any nonunit quasiparticle residue or changed NN interaction/current requires a consistent new prescription.

For labelled complete phase space use S=1/(prod_initial n_species! prod_final n_species!). Each enumerated family has exactly one identical baryon pair, hence S=1/2, in either direction. Exchange interference is part of M and does NOT replace this event-counting factorial. Sum all external spin states with per-spin FD occupations and one active neutrino helicity; add no extra spin2 factors. If a code averages initial spins instead, the corresponding initial degeneracies must be inserted exactly once and independently tested. Normalize antisymmetric states/labelled amplitudes consistently; do not apply an additional exchange-normalization factor twice.

A covariantly normalized comparator uses integral prod[d³p/((2pi)³2E)] (2pi)^4 delta4 times S sum_spins|M|² and direct FD occupation products. An NR quasiparticle calculation may use a different state normalization; its amplitude and phase measure must be derived together. Converting a paper's noncovariant M to this formula by retaining both sets of1/(2E) is invalid. A per-volume net number integral has natural dimensionE4 and converts with1/(hbar hbarc³); its energy moment has dimensionE5 with the same conversion. Absolute normalization is OPEN until the microscopic amplitude and an independent limit match.

For beta emission use F=f_n f_N(1-f_p)(1-f_Nprime)(1-f_l)(1-fanti); B swaps every occupation/hole. The spectator momentum and energy change despite cancelling chemically. Store separate positive/log F and B and signed expm1-stable differences. Lepton-capture/positive-lepton families need their literal crossed weak currents and species products. Derive them rather than mass-substituting a beta tensor. Return separate number, neutrino, lepton and EACH baryon energy moments and direct material loss. Signed material energy is minus radiated energy, without an additional Q term. Test charge, both flavor lepton numbers and entropy before coupling to an element/slab.

## Thermal opening, collision widths and matching

There are three distinct mechanisms/contracts. (1) Zero-width quasiparticles with actual FD tails can open direct-Urca phase space thermally. (2) Explicit two-particle/two-hole strong-scattering diagrams add interacting excitations; exact intermediate propagators may approach an on-shell pole. (3) A dressed-width correlator resums selected collisions and produces spectral support. None is an empirical threshold width fitted to make a plot smooth.

Our policy is to retain alternatives as separately named prescriptions until matching is derived. An off-pole, fixed-order modified calculation can be compared with direct rates where its perturbative domain and nonoverlap are justified. Near an on-shell intermediate state it must reject the unsupported point or use a sourced/resummed prescription; no arbitrary regulator earns a physical accuracy certificate. A width-inclusive total must not have a separate modified rate or already-dressed direct contribution simply added to it. Any subtraction/matching scheme must identify shared self-energy cuts and vertex terms, perturbative order and detailed balance, and be tested numerically. Neutral-current Ward identities require their own consistent vertex treatment.

For an AHZ-style comparison, Wn/Wp are externally supplied interaction-model outputs with declared energy/momentum and full/half-width conventions, not fit-to-answer parameters. Its mass-spectral integration must keep baseline chemical potentials/vector offsets fixed. Rematching density at every spectral mass changes the definition. Preserve the signed-mass integration domain and missing tail weight explicitly; the current positive-mass direct reference cannot automatically cover it. The exact map m=M*+(W/2)tan(theta) gives R(m)dm=dtheta/pi and a useful normalization/zero-width test, but is not a new EOS. Negative-mass support, spectral numerators and W=0 limits need analysis before a numerical wrapper is accepted.

## Concrete next implementable rungs and exit gates

1. **Diagram/algebra rung:** implement two-nucleon spin/isospin states and antisymmetrized leading-NR OPE+weak amplitudes at a handful of fixed, off-pole kinematic points. Resolve Eq23/source normalization first. Independently compare explicit spin sums with a separately derived trace; test exchange sign/permutation, both spectators, reciprocal currents, coupling-zero limits and factorial counting. Do not claim a rate from the current contract alone.
2. **Cold normalized rung:** integrate the declared angular/current model on Fermi surfaces and attach the independently checked energy kernel. Reproduce an exact selected Friman–Maxwell/Yakovlev–Levenfish convention, with all coupling, pairing, correlation, branch and unit choices matched; preserve discrepancies. Compare the existing NMB cold function only within its documented prescription. This is a distinct, useful source-labelled analytic reference and later fit baseline.
3. **Actual-FD off-pole rung:** pick one controlled NR homogeneous state well below a direct threshold, with explicit quasiparticle Hamiltonian and interaction. Use spectator-pair total/relative momenta and physical lepton-energy coordinates; eliminate a momentum delta and solve an exact energy shell with Jacobian/support partitions. Importance-sample actual FD occupations near EACH species' own surface and tails, not a single classical target. Cross-check selected differential points with an independently ordered delta reduction before total number/energy integrals. Use deterministic/adaptive rules on reduced subintegrals and independent seeded sampling only where dimension requires it; retain variance, rare-region and omitted-tail diagnostics.
4. **Pole/matching rung:** audit the denominator minimum over contributing support BEFORE expensive integration. For near-pole cases implement a sourced width-inclusive alternative and demonstrate W→0 recovery away from threshold, spectral normalization/tails, KMS and positive total F/B. Compare different interaction-sourced widths and explicit off-pole calculations; label differences model sensitivity. Do not promise regulator-independent convergence by increasing sample count.
5. **Controlled transport/fits rung:** after absolute spectral and integral gates, expose one-helicity j/chi, number/energy sources and optional opacity moments to a fixed bath/element/slab. Preserve thermodynamic and flavor ledgers. Only then fit declared observables over measured heldouts; freeze interaction/width/domain and report positivity, equilibrium and derivative limits. No fit replaces the detailed chain.

Every rung requires independent review and reproducible forward values before derivative claims. The research uncertainties are NN interaction/short-range and exchange-current choices, quasiparticle spectra, collision-width provenance, diagram matching, degeneracy and pairing—not hydro-engine performance. No production or broader physical-completion claim is made.
