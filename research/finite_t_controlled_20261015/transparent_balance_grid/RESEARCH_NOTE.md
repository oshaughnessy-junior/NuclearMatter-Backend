# Three-bath transparent free-npe composition-balance extension

This is a new selected-state experiment, not a fit or a broader admission certificate. The original T=2 MeV,nB=1e-7 fm^-3 result is preserved read-only. Three new baths are preregistered in CONTRACT.json: (T,nB)=(1,1e-7),(3,1e-7),(2,1e-6), using MeV and fm^-3. Each composition trial solves the actual free relativistic particle-only n,p density roots and charge-neutral pair-equilibrated e± EOS, then evaluates all three physical event integrals. Incoming neutrinos and antineutrinos are exactly empty. Electromagnetic equilibration of the charged pair bath and an external fixed-temperature thermostat are assumed; photon dynamics are absent.

For beta decay n→p+e−+antinue and positron capture n+e+→p+antinue, material net-electron number increases by one per event. Electron capture p+e−→n+nue decreases it. Therefore nB*dYe/dt=Rbeta+RPC−REC, while escaping electron-lepton number is REC−Rbeta−RPC. Charge and baryon number are conserved with Yp=Ye. Material energy loss is the sum of escaped neutrino energies; physical rest masses are already present in every EOS/event energy, so no extra mass-Q term is added. The same reaction currents, form factors, bare weak mass, spin/helicity factors and units as the independently accepted hot source kernels are retained. The independently reviewed PC original event oracle is executed as a physical integral, not used as saved numbers or a rescaling law.

The kinetic root solves the actual integrated number-source balance. The chemical root separately solves mun−mup−mue=0 in the same free EOS. Neither is asserted to be a trapped equilibrium state. Positive escaping energy at a composition root requires a thermostat and is compatible with zero net electron-lepton escape when nue and antinue number emission balance. No energy equilibrium, cooling trajectory, complete weak inventory, interacting EOS, collision width, uncertainty or derivative claim follows.

The coarse/fine event profiles are those of the earlier accepted source-balance experiment, but every new state/trial must independently pass the declared original gates. PC uses moving-neutron tangent momentum and logarithmic positron momentum; EC uses threshold-resolved initial charged-lepton energy and actual proton momentum; beta compares rest-pair/Laguerre-parent with rest-electron/direct-parent coordinates. These are partly shared physical building blocks, not three independent nuclear-model validations. Distinct source quadratures and final isolated upper-tail tests qualify only selected points. All actual species energy moments, separate channel number/energy sources, density roots, quadrature controls and lepton/energy ledgers are retained.

Driver run_state.py launches one new bath per process and checkpoints each completed source pair before checking its gates. Failed/incomplete states stop without adjusting gates. Every source pair computes both physical quadrature profiles, including bracket/root trials, rather than fitting a sampled source. Final root requires normalized drift≤1e-7; physical moment comparisons and final tail comparisons require≤3e-6. The independent review must assess numerical independence and any near-gate cases before acceptance. Fixed-bath opacity/spectrum convenience APIs are never called outside their contracts.

The earlier independent EC/PC/beta acceptance is pinned to establish which exact source implementations and current conventions are being exercised. It does not independently certify the new temperatures/density. The present author forward/root/refinement tests and a different review are required for this expansion. The local admission list allows exactly the three declared baths and .05≤Ye≤.9; it does not imply that every admitted intermediate state has a proved error bound. The .2–.85 kinetic bracket is freshly evaluated with both profiles for each bath; the chemical root uses .05–.9 and is itself followed by an actual two-profile source evaluation.
