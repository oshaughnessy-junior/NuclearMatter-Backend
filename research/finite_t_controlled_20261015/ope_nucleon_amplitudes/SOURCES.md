# Primary sources accessed 2026-10-15

- Friman & Maxwell, *Neutrino emissivities of neutron stars*, ApJ232,541(1979), [original ADS PDF](https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1979ApJ...232..541F), SecIII,Eq10–11 and SecVI. The primary specifies an uncorrelated static momentum-transfer OPE operator, then treats correlations and additional interactions separately. Its quoted pion-pole coupling is `f²=4π×0.08≈1`; this is not exactly our selected `f=1.01`. The OCR of Eq10 is incomplete; its complete transfer structure is independently supported by the explicit derivation below. A direct local curl download failed DNS; no local PDF or invented snapshot is supplied.
- Martin Savage, author lecture derivation, [PHYS560 OPE](https://archive.int.washington.edu/users/mjs5/Class_560/lec560_3/node2.html), Eqs11–15. Explicit derivative pion interaction, static q0=0, negative momentum potential sign and Fourier `d³q/(2π)³`. His pion-decay convention is F=132MeV and coefficient `gA²/(2F²)`; the common F≈92MeV convention instead uses `gA²/(4F²)`. The dimensionless derivative pion-NN coupling here is a different quantity. His coordinate-space discussion identifies the contact distribution contained in the full momentum operator; this leaf does not subtract it or supply fitted contact terms.
- Suleiman, Oertel & Mancini, [2308.09819v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.09819v2), Eqs22–24, AppB/C. Selects `f_piNN=1.01,m_pi=140MeV`. Its Eq23 numerator literally repeats the outgoing momentum index, so that displayed formula is **not** used as an implemented transfer prescription or silently corrected. The present separately derived operator is a named static OPE choice. No claim is made to reproduce its full two-loop diagrams, spin/isospin tables, regulators or opacity normalization.
- Pavon Valderrama & Ruiz Arriola, [nucl-th/0504067](https://arxiv.org/pdf/nucl-th/0504067), SecIIA Eqs5–7 and surrounding parameter definitions. Provides the Goldberger–Treiman parameter conversion `g_piNN=gA M/F` with its F=92.4MeV convention and distinguishes OPE solutions from simple perturbative use. Our Born cross sections are not its nonperturbative deuteron/NN calculation.

All remaining algebra, matrix traces, normalization derivation and numerical values in this leaf are local calculations under the explicitly stated model. Source agreement on operator form does not certify empirical NN scattering accuracy.
