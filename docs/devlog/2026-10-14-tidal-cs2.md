# Dev log — 2026-10-14 — tidal term consistent with the structure's ε(h); h by log-linear quadrature; IAU GM☉

**Who:** R. O'Shaughnessy (with AI pair).  Upstream fix of the defect found by
Q2C's crust–core seam investigation (Q2C PR #47, `notes/35_crust_core_seam.md`)
and refined by the adversarial review of that PR (findings F2, F5).

Three changes.  (1) and (2) are one change on branch claude/tidal-cs2-20261014;
(3) is a separate draft PR, because it moves every mass and a pinned test.

1. **Tidal term.** `(ε+p)/c_s²` in the Love-number equation is now the analytic
   dε/dh of the same Hermite cell that gives ε(h) (it equals `(ε+p) dε/dp`
   exactly, because dp/dh = ε+p in the interpolant).
2. **Pseudo-enthalpy quadrature.** The node h of `build_h_and_cs2` is now
   ∫dp/(ε+p) along the log-linear ε(p) of each cell (12-point Gauss–Legendre),
   not the trapezoid.  Without this, (1) alone makes coarse smooth tables worse.
3. **Units** (separate draft PR).  `GMSUN_KM` is the IAU nominal 1.476625 km; it
   was 1.477004 km (2.57e-4 high).

## Defect 1: the tidal c_s² was not the derivative of the structure's ε(h)

`hermite_eos_at_h` gave ε from a cubic Hermite of p(h) with node slopes ε+p, but
c_s² from node values `np.gradient(p)/np.gradient(eps)` interpolated linearly in
h.  Across any cell, the tidal source `∫(ε+p)/c_s² dh` should equal the cell's
Δε (the smooth limit of the junction condition Δy = −4πr³Δε/(m+4πr³p)).  With
the node c_s² it does not.  Across a stiff joint (p ×2.7 while ε rises 11%, as
at Q2C's SLy-crust/RMF-core seams), the central difference at the last crust
node spans the joint.  The stiff value then leaks into the soft crust cell below
it, and the tidal source misses part of the energy jump: a "phantom" Δε of
−5.1 MeV/fm³ on the steep test table below and −6.0 on Q2C's FSUGold2 gap table.
Λ1.4 then comes out low: −4.7% and −6.2%.

Fix: `hermite_eos_tidal_at_h` / `EOSTable.at_h_tidal` return (ε, p, dε/dh), where
dε/dh = d²p/dh² − dp/dh in the cell.  d²p/dh² is written through the end-slope
excesses over the secant, so the O(s/dh) parts cancel analytically.  ε and p
are bit-identical to `at_h`.  There is no division, and per cell the source
integrates to Δε to roundoff (Gauss–Legendre check: 1e-14).  A safety clip to
[0, 1e10 (ε+p)] guards degenerate cells only (see "Suite comparison").
`EOSTable.cs2` and the c_s² returned by `at_h` are unchanged, kept for
diagnostics such as `tov.central` and the RIFT `cs2_c` column.

## Defect 2: the trapezoid h made the Hermite ε(h) overshoot (structure sector)

The adversarial review of Q2C PR #47 (F2) patched the tidal c_s² alone, as
(dp/dh)/(dε/dh) of the Hermite.  On a 250-node SLy table this moved Λ1.4 *away*
from the oracle: 299.42 to 302.91, against 296.28.  On that table nmbackend was
also off in R1.4 by +41 m and in Mmax by +0.46%, which no c_s² can change.

The cause is the node h.  The Hermite takes its node slopes from dp/dh = ε+p and
its cell widths Δh from the table's h.  The trapezoid Δh = Δp·(1/s₀+1/s₁)/2 is
inconsistent with those slopes by O((Δ ln ε)²) per cell.  In cells where ε
changes much more than p (neutron drip, crust–core joins) the cubic then
overshoots between the nodes.  This is a structure-sector error that does not
shrink with core resolution, because it sits in the crust:

| table | ΔR1.4 trapezoid h | ΔR1.4 log-linear h |
|---|---|---|
| MetaModel default / ×4 / ×16 core nodes | +4.0 / +3.3 / +3.3 m | 0.0 / 0.0 / 0.0 m |
| GM1 (RMFT) n_points 200 / 800 / 3200 | +9.9 / +4.8 / +4.5 m | 0.0 m |
| SLy, 250 / 2000 nodes (Q2C) | +41.3 / +0.6 m | 0.0 m |
| Q2C gap joins (FSUGold2, lr-v1, cont200) | +22 to +32 m | 0.0 m |

(ΔR is against the independent reference below on the same nodes.)

Fix: `build_h_and_cs2(..., h_rule="loglinear")`, the new default.  It is
differentiable, and cells with non-positive ε or p fall back to the trapezoid.
`h_rule="trapezoid"` reproduces the old h.

## Tangents: the tidal term now jumps at the nodes

dε/dh is quadratic inside a cell and jumps at the nodes (the Hermite is only
C¹).  The adaptive solve stops at the nodes with a mesh frozen against
differentiation (devlog 2026-10-12).  A node whose h moves with the EOS
parameters then carries a boundary term (f₊ − f₋)·dh_node/dθ that the frozen
mesh never sees.  Measured with dε/dh in h and no correction, AD/FD of
dΛ/dL_sym is 2.6, and of dΛ/dscale on the steep table 2.5 (7.8 and 11 with
trapezoid h).

**Fix (`tov.solver._derivs_x`).**  y is carried in a node-anchored picture: at
solve coordinate x it holds y(h(x)), where h(x) is piecewise linear with knots
at the gradient-stopped node positions U ↔ the live h.  The nodes then sit at
fixed x for y.
- r and m stay in h.  Their right-hand side is continuous, so they need no
  boundary term, and moving them too costs accuracy: a full reparametrisation
  of (r, m, y) gave dM/dL_sym 5.6e-4 at the default rtol, from the large
  intermediate shift tangent.
- The y equation takes r, m at h(x) to first order, r + (dr/dh)(h(x) − x),
  where h(x) − x is exactly zero in value.
- The cell that holds the frozen start is anchored there, so h(x) = x at the
  start.  At the end, y is moved back to h = x (`_y_to_h`).
- Values are bit-identical to integrating y in h.  Structure-only solves
  (`tidal=False`) are bit-identical to the old solver in values and tangents.

**Second derivatives in EOS parameters were never supported.**
`jacfwd(jacfwd)` of (M, R, Λ) in L_sym, against FD of the AD first derivative,
was off by +4.9, −10 and −890 (AD/FD − 1) on main.  It is now off by +0.96,
−1.4 and −3.5.  The `_adaptive_tov` docstring said "all parameter derivatives
are fine"; it now says first only.  In addition, the y picture is first-order
in the node motion by construction.

**`jump_nodes=False` lost its purpose with tidal=True.**  Without node stops the
adaptive steps straddle the jumps, so the opt-out is no longer cheaper.  Family
values take 81 ms against 88 ms (MetaModel) and 78 ms against 38 ms (GM1).  Λ
is off by 7e-4 (MetaModel) and 6e-3 (GM1) against 2e-7 with the default
(main: 7.7e-4 and 1.8e-4, at 3x and 1.1x less cost than the default).  The
existing `test_jump_nodes_opt_out_is_finite_and_close` (bound 1e-3 at its star)
still passes.  The docstrings now recommend against the opt-out with the tidal
sector.  No downstream caller uses it (Q2C and nicer-hotspot-ad were grepped).

**The `scan` backend** uses the same dε/dh in h with a fixed grid, so its Λ
error is now first order in the step where a step straddles a node.  It was
never the accurate path.

### Found while checking F1: the junction in `solve_hybrid` had the wrong sign
- **The defect.**  `tov.phase_transition.solve_hybrid` and
  `reference.scipy_tov.solve_reference_hybrid` both applied
  y_out = y_in − 4πr³(ε_out − ε_in)/(m+4πr³p), which raises y across the
  density drop.  Their cross-check could not see this, because both had the same
  sign.
- **The correct sign** is y_out = y_in − 4πr³(ε_in − ε_out)/(m+4πr³p), which
  lowers y.  Three independent arguments:

  - Its Newtonian limit is the self-bound-star surface term: Δy = −3 for a

    - with the fixed junction, solve_hybrid gives 97.73;
