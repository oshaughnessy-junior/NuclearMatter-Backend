# Conditional native inner-core thermal integrals

`nmbackend.cooling.physics.native.inner_core_sums` integrates unpaired npe-muon heat capacity and neutrino luminosity on a differentiable spherical TOV profile. It uses the supplied EOS's native log-density/enthalpy map and composition at that density, proper GR volume, and redshift factors for energy and luminosity measured at infinity. It resolves electron and muon direct-Urca onset roots with implicit derivatives and integrates partially occupied radial cells so a moving emitting boundary contributes to the derivative.

This is an inner-core building block. Choose `n_cut` above the EOS's crust remapping region, provide central enthalpy matching the profile, and check saved-profile convergence. Composition must cover the selected density interval. Each channel must have at most one non-tangent opening; sampled re-closing channels are rejected as NaN. Exact contacts with the central density or cut, pairing, crust physics, envelopes and time evolution are outside this interface. No surface-chemical-potential renormalization is applied. This density map is an explicit ansatz, not a universal thermodynamic reconstruction.

Independent tests use the exact constant-density Schwarzschild interior to check proper volume, heat capacity and open/closed unpaired Urca luminosity. Algebraic npe onset and a root-to-partial-shell moving-volume derivative are checked separately. Existing NSCool core-kernel parity tests continue to exercise the shared Urca amplitude.

A coupled metamodel example at L_sym=100 MeV, fixed central h=0.3, T_infinity=3e8 K and n_cut=0.16 fm^-3 passed AD versus finite differences with the unchanged combined tolerance rtol=2e-3, atol=1e-5 in derivatives of log quantities per MeV. TOV rtol=1e-12/atol=1e-14 is needed to stabilize the small finite-difference steps. Profiles with 2048 and 4096 points differ by less than 1.92e-7 in forward values; gradient refinement changes by at most 1.31e-6 absolute. Weak heat-capacity gradients pass primarily through absolute tolerance, not 0.2% relative accuracy. The separately reviewed lepton Hessian repair is required for EOS pressure sensitivities.

`examples/native_core_sensitivity.py` re-solves gravitational mass for each finite-difference perturbation. At the deliberately Urca-enabled baseline mass 1.7817995 solar masses, d ln L_DU / d L_sym is 0.311247 MeV^-1, compared with 0.313793 MeV^-1 at fixed central h. The change of stellar mass therefore explains only about 0.81% of this local sensitivity. The heat-capacity derivative changes sign with the conditioning, but its absolute effect is small. This example uses `MetaModelEOS(muons=True, s_high="power")`: the pressure has a constant-sound-speed tail above the matching density, while composition there is separately continued with a power-law symmetry energy. This is one unpaired metamodel/CSS-tail composition ansatz, not a representative population or observational constraint; gaps, temperature, mass and composition uncertainty can mimic or dominate the response. Re-solved finite differences test differentiation and mass inversion, while sharing the forward physical model.

Dependency base combines reviewed profile PR #28, lepton-Hessian PR #29 and NSCool fixture PR #27. The feature diff is kept separate from those changes. Native whole-star cooling and the unresolved C2 full-range envelope/age gate are not claimed complete.
